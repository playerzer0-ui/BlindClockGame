blind clock game

click clock and wait for the right time, 
then click the clock again to stop it. 
The goal is to stop the clock as close to the target time as possible.

u need timer and a button

at the start, u will see the target time

u click the button to start the clock, and it will begin ticking.

the clock will slowly vanish and the screen will black out, only the button can be seen

click the button if u think the time is right

too early, screen is red, too late, screen is blue, and if you are right, the screen is green.
